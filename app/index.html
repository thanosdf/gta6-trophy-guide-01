    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in the order to play them. Story first, then the people of Leonida, then activities, collections and crime, and the cleanup last. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the other 54 are done.

    Missables

    No trophy in the official list is confirmed missable. Every story trophy unlocks by finishing a mission or a chapter, and the collectibles, counts and activities can all be finished after the story. That is what the official descriptions say, and this guide does not invent a missable where the list shows none.

    Three trophies are on the watch list instead: they need a partner beside you, or a second attempt, so grab them while the chance is there.

    Safe to leave until later

    Everything else can be done after the finale: all 12 story trophies come from playing the story, the collections (cards, WAiNK spots, meditation spots, vehicles, areas, garages) stay open, and the counted tasks (stunts, challenges, races, choices) keep counting.

    About & sources

    This guide is a free, open-source, offline web app. It loads only its own files and works with the network off. No Rockstar logo and no official artwork appear anywhere in it; the look is its own.

    Where the facts come from

    • Exophase (exophase.com) — the official trophy list: every name, description and grade, and the counts the descriptions give (250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages).
    • PSNProfiles (psnprofiles.com) — the PSN counts the list does not spell out, such as the count of 500 tracked for Actions Speak Louder, and community findings for the trophies whose descriptions give no step.
    • My own words — every guide is written for this entry from the official description. Where a description gives no location or step, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing one.

    Your progress

    Ticking a trophy saves it in this browser (localStorage) and nowhere else. Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; import reads one back. Nothing is sent anywhere.
